The Rules — apply everywhere

  • Navy leads, teal accents. Navy is the brand color. Teal is the accent that works on any background — the "Matrix" word, labels, links, confirm buttons.
  • Yellow only on navy. Yellow is a pop — the active nav marker, one key button on a dark panel. Never yellow text on white; it disappears.
  • "Matrix" is the small-caps mark. Monospace, spaced, uppercase, teal. No italic serif — that's retired.
  • Mono means "label." Uppercase Geist Mono with wide letter-spacing marks section labels and metadata only.
  • Warm, not stark. Backgrounds are Paper (#F4F3F1), cards are white. Avoid cold pure-grey.
  • Same brand on exports. The Word doc and the print layout carry this exact palette and type — no exceptions.
  • Rounded, soft-shadowed. Cards use large radii (20px) and soft layered shadows, never hard borders alone.
